How to use it
  • Search on the left, then click a card to drop it in the selected pocket, or drag it straight onto the page.
  • Click a pocket, then shift-click another to select a rectangle. Merge turns it into one panel and the artwork spreads across it. Split undoes that.
  • Drag a card between pockets to swap them.
  • Blank marks a pocket as left empty on purpose, which is part of the method rather than an oversight.
  • Upload puts your own image in a pocket, for printed inserts.
  • Shortcuts: M merge, S split, B blank, Delete clear, Esc deselect, ← → pages, Ctrl+F search.
  • Everything saves in this browser as you go. Export writes a file you can keep or move; Copy link makes a link that carries the design, though not your uploads.
  • Printing lays pockets flush at 63×88 mm with cut guides, so a panel stays continuous across the cards you cut.
