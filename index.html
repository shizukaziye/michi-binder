How to use it
  • Search on the left, then click a card to drop it in the selected pocket, or drag it straight onto the page.
  • Drag a card or insert to another pocket to swap them.
  • Click a pocket to select it. To pick a group, drag from an empty pocket across the others and let go — the selection only changes while you hold. To take in pockets that already have cards, click one and shift-click another. Merge turns the group into one panel and the artwork spreads across it. Split undoes that.
  • Name a page in the Name this page box — the name shows above the page and in its chip's tooltip. Two pages side by side: Spread view shows a binder opening (pages 2 – 3, 4 – 5…). Drag the numbered page chips to reorder pages.
  • Made a mistake? Undo and Redo step through every edit (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z).
  • Combine → lays the next page beside this one as one wide spread (say two 3×3 pages into a 6×3). It is wider than A4 — print on A3, or use "Fit to page".
  • Blank marks a pocket as left empty on purpose, which is part of the method rather than an oversight.
  • Under Your inserts, drop an image, paste one, or drop it straight onto the page. Choose how many pockets it should fill, frame it, and it joins your library — then drag it onto a page like a card and it takes exactly that many pockets.
  • Shortcuts: M merge, S split, B blank, Delete clear, Esc deselect, Ctrl+Z undo, ← → pages, Ctrl+F search.
  • Everything saves in this browser as you go. Export writes a file you can keep or move; Copy link makes a link that carries the design, though not your uploads.
  • Printing lays pockets flush at 63×88 mm with cut guides, so a panel stays continuous across the cards you cut.
